# drafts/

Unpublished launch and marketing material. **Nothing in here is shipped content.**

This directory holds working drafts — announcement copy, post bodies, positioning
notes, prepared answers — that exist so they can be reviewed and revised *before*
they are posted somewhere irreversible. None of it is built by Hugo, served from
the website, or referenced by the compiler.

Rules of thumb:

- Nothing here is published automatically. Posting is always a manual, human act.
- Treat every file as a draft until the owner says otherwise, even if it reads finished.
- Text here may describe behaviour that is aspirational or not yet fixed. Do not
  cite `drafts/` as documentation. `docs/` and `website/content/` are the sources of truth.

Current contents:

- `launch/show-hn.md` — pre-launch audit plus ready-to-paste copy for Show HN and
  r/ProgrammingLanguages (GitHub issue #105).
